# Claim boundaries

- A wallet surfaced for review is not a confirmed Sybil. A reconstructed cluster does not establish common control, intent, or misconduct.
- Low or absent accepted evidence does not clear a wallet. Findings are limited to the observed source coverage and time window.
- Evidence-family occurrences can overlap; they are not independent proofs, distinct wallets, or additional edges.
- Complete acquisition describes provider-window coverage for the stated population, not independent verification of all chain activity.
- Excluding Passport scores, known Sybil labels, and external wallet classifications from analytical input does not establish strict experimental blindness.
- No ground-truth detection performance, superiority over Passport or matching mechanisms, or comparative count of confirmed Sybils is established.
- File-integrity checks establish byte consistency, not semantic correctness or classification truth. The recorded verification counts describe the frozen checks; the release checksums cover the documents provided here. These aggregate documents alone do not enable independent reconstruction of wallet-level findings.
